## A dedicated accounting partner for Hartford SD businesses

Most CPA firms show up once a year, hand you a tax return, and disappear until next April. BussCPA works differently. Our outsourced accounting services are built to function as your accounting department year-round, not a once-a-year appointment.

Hartford SD business owners in construction, agriculture, and dozens of other trades need more than a tax preparer. They need someone answering questions in March as readily as in April. Our outsourced accounting services give you a fixed monthly fee, secure digital workflows, and real-time access to your financial data, so you always know where your business stands. Whether you run a contracting crew, manage a farm operation, or need personal tax help for your household, this page walks through exactly how BussCPA supports you.

## Outsourced accounting that gives you real-time visibility

Bookkeeping shouldn't feel like a black box. BASE, our end-to-end outsourced accounting service, replaces the shoebox-of-receipts approach with secure digital workflows and a client portal you can log into any time you want an answer.

Every transaction is recorded, categorized, and reconciled on a schedule, not scrambled together weeks after the fact. You get real-time online access to your books, so a question about cash on hand or last month's job costs gets answered by logging in, not by waiting on a callback.

Pricing is fixed and monthly. No surprise invoices tied to how many questions you asked or how many hours a project took. Hartford SD business owners budgeting for accounting costs know exactly what's coming out of the account each month, which is one less variable to manage in a business that already has plenty of them.

## Advisory support that helps you make better business decisions

Bookkeeping tells you what already happened. Advisory work is where BussCPA helps you plan what happens next. Once your books are current and accurate, real strategic conversations become possible: cash flow forecasting, pricing decisions, equipment purchase timing, and profitability by job or by division.

For a contractor deciding whether to bid on a bigger project or a farm operation weighing a land purchase against equipment financing, that conversation matters more than a tax form filed in April. Advisory sessions are built around your numbers, not generic benchmarks pulled from an industry report.

Owners who use BASE alongside advisory support get a CPA who already understands their books, since it's the same team handling both. There's no re-explaining your business to a new advisor every time a decision needs to get made.

## Personal income tax preparation for individuals and households

Not every client is a business. BussCPA prepares personal income tax returns for individuals and households in and around Hartford SD, with the same digital-first approach used across the firm. You upload documents securely through a client portal instead of dropping off a folder and hoping nothing gets lost.

What that looks like for you:

- Secure upload of W-2s, 1099s, and supporting documents from any device
- Direct communication with the preparer working on your return, not a call center
- Year-round availability for tax planning questions, not just a February-to-April window
- Accuracy checks built into the digital workflow before anything is filed
- Coordination with your business return if you also own a company BussCPA serves

Tax season doesn't have to mean rearranging your schedule around office hours. It shouldn't mean guessing whether your preparer is even available in July when a life event changes your tax picture.

## Specialized accounting for construction, agriculture, and other niche industries

Generic bookkeeping misses the details that matter in specialized industries. A local tax prep shop that fills out the same form for every walk-in client isn't set up to catch job costing errors on a construction project or seasonal payroll swings on a farm. BussCPA builds its service around the industries it knows.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Job costing, retainage, equipment depreciation, and bonding requirements all affect a contractor's bottom line differently than a standard small business. BussCPA tracks profitability by job so you know which projects are actually making money.

### Agriculture
icon: Wrench

Farm operations have payroll and tax needs that look nothing like a retail business, from seasonal labor to equipment financing to commodity price swings. BussCPA structures accounting and payroll around the agricultural calendar, not a generic monthly template.

### Attorneys
icon: Scale

Trust accounting rules and client fund handling require precision most bookkeepers never encounter. BussCPA understands the compliance stakes attached to law firm finances.

### Engineers
icon: Cog

Project-based billing, multi-year contracts, and R&D considerations shape how engineering firms should track revenue and expenses. BussCPA builds reporting around project timelines, not just calendar quarters.

### Family Offices and Trusts
icon: ShieldCheck

Multigenerational wealth and trust structures need accounting that accounts for beneficiaries, distributions, and long-term tax planning. BussCPA supports family offices and trusts across South Dakota with that level of detail.
