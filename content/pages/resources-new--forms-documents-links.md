Every tax season, mailboxes and inboxes across Hartford fill up with W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, and reminder emails from three different software companies. This page exists so BussCPA clients don't have to dig through old email threads or a drawer of manila folders to figure out what they actually need.

Every form and checklist below connects to the same secure client portal BussCPA uses for [outsourced accounting](/services/outsourced-accounting), payroll, and personal tax work. Upload a document once and it lands in your file automatically, no faxing, no re-emailing an attachment that never came through the first time. Whether you're an individual filer in Hartford, a contractor tracking job costs, or a family office managing trust distributions, your forms are organized by category below.

## Personal income tax forms and checklists

Individual and household filers need a handful of documents on hand before a return can move forward. Most clients already have these sitting in a folder or an email inbox by mid-February; this list just makes sure nothing gets left out.

- W-2 wage statements from every employer
- 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, and 1099-R forms for contract work, interest, dividends, and retirement distributions
- Form 1098 for mortgage interest and property tax
- Childcare, tuition, and medical expense receipts
- Prior-year tax return for reference
- Records of charitable contributions and IRA or HSA contributions

Once you've gathered these, skip the printer. Log into the BussCPA client portal and upload everything directly to your [personal income tax](/services/personal-income-tax) file, where your preparer can see it in real time.

## Business and industry-specific tax resources

A generic tax checklist doesn't work for a business owner in Hartford running equipment, payroll, and seasonal cash flow all at once. BussCPA organizes business documents by industry because a contractor's forms look nothing like a farm's.

### Construction and Contractors
icon: Hammer

Job costing reports, 1099s issued to subcontractors, equipment depreciation schedules, and Form 2553 for S-corp elections are the documents that come up most often for [construction](/industries/construction) clients. BussCPA tracks these against project timelines, not just calendar quarters.

### Agriculture and Farming
icon: Coins

Schedule F, farm income averaging elections, and equipment depreciation under Section 179 all carry different rules than a standard small business return. [Agriculture](/industries/agriculture) clients also have payroll and withholding needs that shift with planting and harvest seasons, and BussCPA builds document requests around that calendar instead of a generic monthly schedule.

### Automotive Businesses
icon: Wrench

Parts inventory records, floor plan financing statements, and technician payroll documentation are standard requests for automotive shops and dealerships. BussCPA pulls these together with the same monthly cadence used for construction and agriculture clients, so nothing falls through the cracks between busy seasons.

## Resources for attorneys, engineers, and family offices

Professional service firms and family offices carry documentation needs that a general small-business checklist won't cover. BussCPA works with these niches directly and knows which forms tend to get missed.

### Attorneys
icon: Gavel

Trust accounting statements, IOLTA reconciliations, and entity-level K-1s are common documents for [attorney](/industries/attorneys) clients, particularly those running a professional corporation or partnership alongside individual filings.

### Engineers
icon: Cog

R&D credit documentation, contract retainage schedules, and equipment depreciation records come up frequently for [engineering](/industries/engineers) firms billing on a project basis. BussCPA keeps these organized by project rather than by calendar month.

### Family Offices and Trusts
icon: Building2

Form 1041 for trust income, K-1s for beneficiaries, and gift tax documentation are the recurring pieces for [family office and trust](/industries/family-offices-trusts) clients. Multi-generational reporting requires a level of organization a shoebox of receipts can't provide.
