## Accounting built around how your industry works

Bookkeeping software can log a transaction. It can't tell you why your job costs ran over budget in June, or how South Dakota's agricultural payroll rules affect your third-quarter withholding. BussCPA built its outsourced accounting services package around industries we already understand: construction, agriculture, law, engineering, and family wealth management.

Every BASE client pays a fixed monthly fee, so there's no surprise invoice when your books get more complicated during a busy season. You get secure digital workflows and real-time online access to your financial data, checked from a phone or laptop on your own schedule. We've invested in the technology infrastructure so you don't have to build one in-house.

Hartford, SD businesses don't need someone to log entries. They need an outsourced accounting department that understands job costing, seasonal cash flow, and industry-specific tax rules from the first month. That's what BussCPA delivers, whether you run a construction crew, a grain operation, or a law practice.

## Construction accounting that keeps your projects on budget

Contractors lose money on paper long before they lose it in the field. Without accurate job costing, a general contractor in Hartford or Sioux Falls can look profitable on a monthly P&L while a single job quietly bleeds cash. BussCPA builds job costing systems that track labor, materials, and subcontractor costs against each project, not just the business as a whole.

We also handle work-in-progress reporting, so over-billing and under-billing show up before they become a bonding problem or a bank covenant issue. Construction cash flow moves differently than most small businesses: draw schedules, retainage, and seasonal slowdowns all factor in. Our outsourced accounting team functions as your back office, tracking payables, running payroll across job sites, and delivering real-time numbers instead of a shoebox handed over in April. [Learn more about our construction accounting services](/industries/construction).

## Agriculture accounting and tax planning for South Dakota operations

Farm and ranch operations run on a different clock than most businesses, and the accounting needs to match. Seasonal income concentrated into a few months a year changes how you plan estimated taxes, manage input costs, and time equipment purchases. Agricultural payroll adds another layer: seasonal and family labor, different withholding treatment, and reporting rules that don't apply to a retail shop or an office.

BussCPA works with South Dakota farm operations to build cash flow projections around planting and harvest cycles, structure entity elections that fit multi-generational ownership, and plan depreciation on equipment and land improvements before year-end instead of after. Section 179 elections, commodity contracts, and land lease income all get handled inside the same fixed monthly fee as the rest of your books. [See how we support agriculture clients](/industries/agriculture).

## Personal income tax and household financial support

Not every client who walks through our door owns a business. Hartford families and individuals come to BussCPA for the same reason business owners do: they want a straight answer and a return filed correctly the first time. We handle W-2 filers, retirees managing required distributions, and households juggling rental property or investment income.

For business owners, personal and business tax planning happen together instead of in separate silos with separate deadlines. A construction company owner's personal return reflects decisions made in the business all year, and we're already tracking both sides. That coordination is hard to get from a firm that only sees your business books or only sees your 1040.
