## Local CPAs who understand Hartford SD businesses

BussCPA is based in Hartford, South Dakota, not routed through a call center three states away. When a construction owner calls about a draw schedule or a farmer calls about a machinery trade during planting season, they reach someone who already knows the local calendar, the local lenders, and the local tax deadlines that matter here.

We built the firm around a simple idea: outsourced accounting should feel like an in-house department, minus the overhead of hiring one. That means real people answering real questions, backed by secure digital tools that let clients see their numbers from a phone, a job site, or a farmhouse kitchen table. Local relationships. Modern systems. That combination is what sets an outsourced accounting department apart from a bookkeeping subscription.

## Experience across construction, agriculture, and professional services

Generalist bookkeeping works fine until the work gets specific. Job costing on a construction project, depreciation schedules on farm equipment, or trust accounting for a family office each demand a different playbook. Our team has built those playbooks around five industries we work in every week.

### Construction Companies and Contractors
icon: Hammer

Job costing, retainage, equipment depreciation, and cash flow timing tied to draw schedules. We track project profitability the way a general contractor needs to see it, not the way a generic chart of accounts assumes.

### Agriculture Enterprises
icon: Cog

Farm and ranch operations run on a different payroll and tax calendar than most businesses. We handle seasonal labor, equipment purchases, and commodity income with that reality built in.

### Attorneys
icon: Gavel

Trust accounting, client fund handling, and partnership tax structures require precision. We support law firms with bookkeeping and tax work that respects those compliance boundaries.

### Engineers
icon: Wrench

Project-based revenue recognition and multi-entity structures are common in engineering firms. We keep the books aligned with how the work actually gets billed.

### Family Offices and Trusts
icon: ShieldCheck

Multi-generational wealth needs accounting that tracks entities, distributions, and tax exposure across family members, not just a single balance sheet.

## Your outsourced accounting department, not just a vendor

Hiring a bookkeeper solves one problem. Hiring an outsourced accounting department solves the whole stack: bookkeeping, payroll, financial statements, advisory, and tax, all under one fixed monthly fee instead of a pile of hourly invoices.

That's the outsourced accounting services model. Instead of assigning one person to your account, we put a team behind it: an accounting specialist handling day-to-day transactions, an advisor watching the bigger financial picture, and a tax preparer making sure decisions made in June don't create surprises in April. You get a predictable cost and a group of people who already know your business when you call, not a rotating cast of contractors.

For construction companies, agriculture operations, attorneys, and family offices, that structure matters more than it does for a simple retail shop. The stakes are higher, the filings are more complex, and a single generalist rarely covers all of it well.

## Specialists who know agriculture's unique payroll and tax needs

Agriculture payroll doesn't follow the same rules as a retail or office payroll run. Seasonal workers, family labor, equipment trades, and commodity sales each carry different tax treatment, and getting them wrong costs real money at filing time. Our team handles these details as a matter of routine, not a special request.

- Seasonal and part-time farm labor payroll, including withholding rules unique to agricultural employers
- Equipment purchases, trades, and depreciation schedules specific to farm machinery
- Commodity income and crop insurance proceeds reported correctly on tax returns
- Coordination between farm entities and personal income tax filings for owners
- Year-round advisory so tax decisions get made before harvest, not after
