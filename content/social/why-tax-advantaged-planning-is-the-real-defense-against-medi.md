## LinkedIn

A single overnight hospital stay in South Dakota can run $3,000 to $10,000, often before insurance finishes adjudicating the claim. Most financial advice stops at "build an emergency fund," but cash sitting in savings doesn't change how the IRS treats a medical bill.

An HSA does. Contributions are deductible, growth is tax-free, and withdrawals for qualified expenses are never taxed, at any age. For 2024, that's up to $4,150 for individuals and $8,300 for families, with no use-it-or-lose-it deadline like an FSA carries.

For construction crews dealing with jobsite injuries or agriculture operations riding out a mid-harvest health scare, that structure matters more than a cushion ever will. The itemized medical deduction can also help in a year when unreimbursed costs cross 7.5% of AGI.
