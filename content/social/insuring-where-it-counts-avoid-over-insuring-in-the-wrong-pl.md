## LinkedIn

A contractor bidding $600,000 jobs today but still carrying liability limits sized for $150,000 jobs from three years ago is one job-site incident away from a gap that outstrips the policy.

That's the kind of mismatch we see across automotive shops, construction crews, and agriculture operations in and around Hartford, SD. The fix isn't buying more insurance across the board, it's running the numbers: your largest quantifiable exposure minus what you can absorb out of pocket, calculated from your actual balance sheet and cash flow, not a generic industry checklist.

Agriculture clients face this differently too. Seasonal payroll and equipment that shifts between owned and leased status change what workers' comp and property coverage should actually look like.

If a policy hasn't been reviewed since your last contract, loan payoff, or trust formation, it's due for a look.
