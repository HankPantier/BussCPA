## LinkedIn

A $60,000 down payment on a $300,000 home stops earning any market return the moment it hits escrow. That's the piece most rent-vs-buy calculators skip, along with the maintenance reserve of 1-2% of home value a year and the fact that most buyers need three to five years in a house before ownership beats renting once closing costs and lost investment returns are factored in. The mortgage interest deduction rarely tips the scale either. A couple paying roughly $16,000 in interest on a $250,000 loan at 6.5% may still land under the standard deduction. Agriculture households with seasonal cash flow and construction owners with variable draws need a different break-even analysis than a salaried engineer, and a national calculator can't account for that. Our latest post walks through the full framework we use with clients across South Dakota, with the exact numbers behind each factor.
