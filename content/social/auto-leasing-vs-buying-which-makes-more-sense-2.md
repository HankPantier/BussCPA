## LinkedIn

A construction client sat on a lease-or-buy decision for six weeks before walking into our Hartford office with an F-150 question. The dealership pitch focused on monthly payment. The real math involves Section 179 deductions, mileage caps of 10,000 to 15,000 miles a year, and what happens to cash flow over the next three to five years. Drive past that lease cap and you're paying 15 to 30 cents per extra mile at lease-end, a number that adds up fast for a crew running multiple trucks. For construction and agriculture operations especially, business-use percentage has to be tracked carefully before any deduction gets claimed. The framework runs through four questions before you talk to a dealer: business-use percentage, annual mileage, deduction timing, and your balance sheet three years out. Read the full breakdown and run your own numbers: https://busscpa.us/resources/auto-leasing-vs-buying-which-makes-more-sense-2
