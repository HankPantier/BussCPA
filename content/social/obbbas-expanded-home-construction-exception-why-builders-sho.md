## LinkedIn

A general contractor doing $45 million in average annual revenue used to be locked into percentage-of-completion accounting on every non-home-construction job. OBBBA just raised the small contractor threshold under IRC 460 from roughly $31 million to $80 million, and that same contractor can now choose completed contract accounting instead.

The difference isn't abstract. On a $1.8 million custom home contract spanning two tax years, PCM can force tax on 55% of projected profit before the house closes or the final draw arrives. Completed contract accounting waits until the home actually sells.

The change applies to contracts signed after enactment, so calendar-year builders see the full effect starting with 2026 contracts. If your construction company runs related entities, like a land development LLC or equipment leasing arm, aggregation rules complicate the gross receipts test, and that's not something to sort out after the contract is signed.

Full breakdown, including the Form 3115 mechanics: https://busscpa.us/resources/obbbas-expanded-home-construction-exception-why-builders-sho
