## LinkedIn

Rent your lake cabin for 14 days a year and the IRS lets you keep every dollar tax-free, no Schedule E required. Cross into 15 days and the rules change fast: personal use vs. rental days determines whether you land in a capped-deduction vacation home category or a full rental property where losses and depreciation actually offset other income. For owners of a place on Lake Madison or in the Black Hills, that day count is the whole ballgame, and getting it wrong after the fact costs more than doing it right from day one. If land near the property is used for hunting leases or agriculture, the accounting gets more layered too. We break down the exact thresholds, the $750,000 mortgage interest limit, and the passive loss rules in the full post.
