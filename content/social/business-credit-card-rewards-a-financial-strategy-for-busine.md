## LinkedIn

A construction contractor running $40,000 a month through a fuel and materials card on flat 1% cash back is leaving money on the table every single cycle. Run the math on $300,000 in annual spend: a flat 1.5% card returns $4,500, but a category card paying 3% on your top two spend categories returns $6,750. That's $2,250 a year, close to two months of part-time bookkeeping, sitting unused because the card doesn't match the spend pattern.

Agriculture operations face a sharper version of this problem. Seasonal spending on seed, feed, and equipment parts means a card built around monthly thresholds can cut your rewards rate in half during slow months.

Rewards only work as a strategy when the card structure and the bookkeeping move together. Read the full breakdown, including how to reconcile rebates versus taxable bonuses: https://busscpa.us/resources/business-credit-card-rewards-a-financial-strategy-for-busine
