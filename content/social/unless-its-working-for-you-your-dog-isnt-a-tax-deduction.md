## LinkedIn

A client last spring argued his shop dog's food and vet bills counted as a business expense because the dog slept in the office every night. The IRS doesn't see it that way. Pet ownership alone has never qualified as a deductible cost, but that doesn't mean dogs are off the table for construction and agriculture clients.

A guard dog kenneled on a jobsite with real theft risk, or a livestock guardian dog protecting a flock from coyotes, can have food, vet care, and training deducted as an ordinary business expense under IRS Publication 535. The line comes down to documentation: site photos, training records, and vet invoices that describe the dog's actual role, not just "annual checkup."

Service animals for a disability fall under Publication 502 as a medical expense instead, with their own itemization rules.
