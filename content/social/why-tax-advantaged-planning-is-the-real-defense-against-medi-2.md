## LinkedIn

A torn ACL on the job site or a farm accident during harvest doesn't wait for your emergency fund to catch up. For 2025, HSA contribution limits sit at $4,300 for self-only coverage and $8,550 for family coverage, and that money is deductible going in, grows tax-free, and comes out tax-free for qualified care. Construction owners running high-deductible plans to keep premiums low should be maxing this out. Agriculture households juggling off-farm coverage or marketplace plans need a different playbook, since payroll and tax treatment around that coverage rarely fits a generic answer. Add in the itemized medical deduction: once unreimbursed costs clear 7.5% of your AGI, a household earning $90,000 crosses that line at $6,750. That's real money back if you've kept the receipts. Read the full breakdown and build the plan before the bill arrives, not after: https://busscpa.us/resources/why-tax-advantaged-planning-is-the-real-defense-against-medi-2
