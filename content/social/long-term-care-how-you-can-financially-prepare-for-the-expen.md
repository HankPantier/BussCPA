## LinkedIn

A private room in a skilled nursing facility commonly runs over $100,000 a year, and Medicare doesn't cover it the way most people assume. That number catches farm families and business owners off guard because the money is usually tied up in land, equipment, or the business itself, not sitting in a checking account.

The households that handle this well aren't guessing. They combine three funding sources years in advance: long-term care insurance purchased while still insurable (typically mid-50s to early 60s), VA Aid and Attendance benefits for eligible veterans and spouses, and a self-funding reserve built into an investment strategy.

For agriculture clients, this intersects with succession planning and how machinery or grain contracts get valued for benefit eligibility. Waiting until a health scare forces the decision limits options and raises the cost.
