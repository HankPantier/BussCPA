## LinkedIn

A volunteer firefighter who drives to three calls a week and buys his own boots has spent real money the IRS will let him deduct, even though his time on scene never counts. The rule most people miss: mileage for volunteer work is 14 cents per mile, and 1,500 miles a year adds up to a $210 deduction on its own, before you count supplies, uniforms, or overnight travel for a board conference. Board members for grain co-ops, trade associations, and family trusts hit this often, and they're also the ones most likely to cross the $250 threshold that requires a written acknowledgment from the organization before filing. If you volunteer, sit on a board, or handle finances for one, this is worth ten minutes of reading before tax season.
