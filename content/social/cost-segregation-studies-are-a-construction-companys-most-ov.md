## LinkedIn

A general contractor who just finished a $1.2 million pole building and yard improvement project in eastern South Dakota could reasonably reclassify 20% to 35% of that cost out of 39-year property. That's the math behind cost segregation studies, and construction companies are better positioned to use them than almost any other business type.

Contractors already track job costs by task: labor hours, material invoices, subcontractor draws. That documentation is exactly what a study needs to substantiate reclassifying wiring, paving, and site lighting into 5, 7, or 15-year property. Paired with bonus depreciation, currently 40% in 2025 and dropping to 20% in 2026, the timing matters right now.

If your company finished a shop, fabrication bay, or yard project this year, this is worth a planning conversation before year-end.
