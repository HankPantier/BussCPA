## LinkedIn

A $429-a-month lease looks better than a $612 loan payment, until you factor in the 12,000-mile cap and $0.25-per-mile overage fee that can turn into a $2,000 surprise for a contractor running between job sites in Lincoln County.

The math changes again for anyone buying a truck over 6,000 pounds GVWR. That vehicle may qualify for Section 179 or bonus depreciation, a deduction leasing simply doesn't offer, which is why a farm buying a grain truck ahead of harvest usually comes out ahead by owning rather than leasing.

Mileage habits, how long you keep the vehicle, and your tax situation all belong in this decision before the payment does. Our latest post walks through the full framework for construction and agriculture owners deciding between the two: https://busscpa.us/resources/auto-leasing-vs-buying-which-makes-more-sense
