## LinkedIn

OBBBA just raised the small contractor threshold from roughly $30 million to $80 million in average gross receipts. That means residential builders and spec developers who were locked into percentage-of-completion accounting may now qualify for completed contract or cash method under the IRC 460 home construction exception. For a builder finishing a $1.2 million custom home over 14 months, that's the difference between owing tax at the 60% completion mark and paying tax closer to when cash from the sale actually lands.

The catch: this applies to contracts signed after enactment, so calendar-year builders need to plan their method election before 2026 contracts get signed, not after. Existing contracts stay locked into whatever method was elected at signing.
