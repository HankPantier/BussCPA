## LinkedIn

200 hours a season coaching youth wrestling. That's what one Hartford client puts in every year, and he asked us if any of it was deductible. It isn't. The IRS has never allowed a deduction for the value of your time, no matter your profession or hours logged. But the mileage to tournaments, the uniforms he buys for kids, and the entry fees he covers? Those can count under IRS Publication 526, and most volunteers never claim them.

This matters most for clients who serve on nonprofit boards or drive for 4-H and ag extension programs around Minnehaha County, where charitable mileage adds up fast over a year. Once a single expense hits $250, you need written acknowledgment from the organization to back it up.
