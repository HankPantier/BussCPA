## LinkedIn

A new $800,000 equipment shop with self-performed build-out can generate $70,000 to $110,000 in extra first-year depreciation through a cost segregation study. Most contractors never ask for one because they assume it's a real estate investor's tool for apartment complexes, not something that applies to a shop, yard, or equipment barn they already own.

Construction companies are actually better candidates than most real estate buyers. You own more categories of qualifying property, you often self-perform work that generates detailed cost records, and you carry higher effective tax rates because so much income flows through as ordinary income.

Bonus depreciation is stepping down each year, so timing the study matters as much as doing it. Read the breakdown, including real dollar scenarios: https://busscpa.us/resources/cost-segregation-studies-are-a-construction-companys-most-ov-2

## Facebook

Own a shop, yard, or heavy equipment? You might be sitting on $150,000 or more in accelerated depreciation you haven't claimed. Cost segregation studies break a building into components that qualify for 5, 7, or 15-year depreciation instead of 39, and construction companies are especially strong candidates because of the detailed cost records they already keep. Bonus depreciation is stepping down each year, so the timing matters.
