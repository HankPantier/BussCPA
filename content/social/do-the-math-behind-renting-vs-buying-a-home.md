## LinkedIn

A $56,000 down payment on a $280,000 home isn't just a purchase, it's a decision to stop that money earning anywhere else. Our latest post runs the actual numbers for a Hartford-area home: 20% down, a 30-year fixed at 6.5%, against a comparable rental at $1,650 a month. Year one, renting looks about $40 cheaper on a pure cash basis. That comparison skips the roughly $310 a month in principal building equity, and it skips what that $56,000 could return if it were invested instead.

For agriculture and construction households with lumpy income, there's a second variable: a mortgage payment has to survive your worst month, not your average one.

We build these projections for advisory clients using real numbers, not a five-minute online calculator. Full breakdown here: https://busscpa.us/resources/do-the-math-behind-renting-vs-buying-a-home
