## LinkedIn

The home construction exception threshold just moved from about $31 million to $80 million in average annual gross receipts under OBBBA. That single number determines whether a builder can use the completed contract method or gets locked into percentage-of-completion, paying tax on framing and drywall months before a closing check arrives. A builder averaging $45 million in gross receipts was required to use percentage-of-completion under prior law. Now they sit well under the new $80 million line and can consider deferring income until the contract closes.

For homebuilders and developers working spec homes across Minnehaha and Turner counties, this changes the math on every contract signed in 2026. Sign before checking your three-year average, and you could lock a contract into a method you no longer need for its entire life.
